SyncIt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

SyncIt does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

All data created within the app, including reminders, todo lists, notes, and checklists, is stored locally on the user's device.

The app does not use internet connectivity, analytics services, or third-party tracking tools.

Users may choose to back up their data locally on their device storage. This data is not transmitted to any external servers.
